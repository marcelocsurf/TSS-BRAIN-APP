01 — What the method is

Surfing, organised so it can be learned.

Most surfers train on intuition: they paddle out hoping for a good session, without knowing their real level or what today’s work is. The Surf Sequence replaces hope with structure — the same way martial arts did — without losing what surfing is: play.

Belts

6 verifiable levels

From White to Black. You advance by verified competence, never by sessions attended.

Sequences

23 sequences

Skill broken into ordered pieces: what to learn, in what order, and what unlocks next.

Pillars

4 pillars

Physical · Technical · Tactical · Mental. The bottom turn that fails is not always a bottom turn problem.

Missions

One intention per session

Every session has a mission, a dose and a review. The prize is executing it — not the perfect wave.

02 — How it works

A map for the journey. A loop for every session.

The map

Where am I, where am I going, what do I need, how do I train it. You revisit it every few months.

The loop

Seven steps you run every time you paddle out. Without the map, the loop is activity without direction. Without the loop, the map is a wish.

Two valid paths

Competitor and personal progression. Same belts, same method — only what you are building toward changes.

01 Mission One intention for the session — a detail, a manoeuvre, or a score-building mission.
02 Reality Look before you enter: conditions, crowd, energy, your own state.
03 Calibrate Match the task and your expectations to your level and the day.
04 Presence The breath, the ritual — leave on the beach whatever you carried from the day.
05 Practice A concrete dose: this many waves, this many attempts, this much time.
06 Review Did the thing you came to do happen? Rate your focus. If something failed — which pillar?
07 Adapt One decision for next time: repeat, progress, simplify, adjust, change, reassess — or advance.
03 — The levels

Six belts. One honest address.

More than one in three surfers place themselves at least one level above their real performance — internal data, 3,000+ surfers assessed. Honesty here is the foundation of everything after.
